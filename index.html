How Each Calculator Works

A quick explainer for every tool in ToolsHub — the formula it uses, and when it's actually useful.

Age Calculator

Enter a birth date and the calculator returns your exact age in years, months, and days, plus the total number of days you've been alive. It accounts for leap years automatically, so the result is precise even across February 29th. It's commonly used for filling out forms that ask for an exact age rather than just "years old," checking eligibility cut-offs, or working out someone's age on a specific future or past date.

BMI Calculator

Body Mass Index is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared (kg/m²). The tool accepts either metric or imperial units and converts automatically. The result is placed into the standard World Health Organization categories: underweight, normal, overweight, and obese. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis — it doesn't account for muscle mass, so it's most useful as a quick reference point, not a medical verdict.

Loan Calculator

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate, and term in months or years, and the calculator returns your fixed monthly payment using the standard amortization formula, along with the total interest paid over the life of the loan. It's useful for comparing offers from different lenders before you commit, or for seeing how much a shorter term versus a lower rate actually saves you.

Percentage Calculator

Handles the three most common percentage questions in one tool: what is X% of Y, what percentage is X of Y, and what is the percentage increase or decrease between two numbers. Useful for discounts, grading, splitting a restaurant bill, or working out year-over-year change without doing the arithmetic by hand.

Tip Calculator

Enter the bill total, choose a tip percentage (or enter a custom one), and split the total across any number of people. The calculator shows the tip amount, the grand total, and the per-person share side by side, which is faster than working it out on a phone's basic calculator at the table.

Temperature Converter

Converts instantly between Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin using the standard conversion formulas. Handy for reading a recipe written in the "wrong" unit, checking a weather forecast from another country, or converting a scientific measurement without looking up the formula each time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to use the tools?

No. Every calculator on ToolsHub and the AI PDF Reader can be used without signing up or providing an email address.

Is my data stored anywhere?

Calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Uploaded PDFs are processed to generate an answer to your question; see our Privacy Policy for details on retention.

Why does the site show ads?

Ads are how we keep every tool free with no subscription. We only use standard, reputable ad networks and don't use pop-ups, forced redirects, or push-notification-based advertising.

Can I use these tools on mobile?

Yes. Every page is built to work on phones and tablets as well as desktop browsers.

How accurate is the BMI or loan calculator?

Both use the standard published formulas (WHO's BMI formula and the standard amortization formula for loans), so results match what you'd get with a manual calculation. BMI is a general screening figure, not medical advice.
