Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to nkr21.online and its subdomains, including tools.nkr21.online (ToolsHub) and pdf.nkr21.online (AI PDF Reader). It explains what information is collected when you use these sites and how it's handled.

2. Calculator inputs

The numbers you enter into any ToolsHub calculator (age, BMI, loan, percentage, tip, or temperature) are processed directly in your browser using JavaScript. They are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

3. PDF uploads

When you upload a PDF to the AI PDF Reader, the document's text is extracted and sent to a third-party AI provider (OpenRouter) to generate answers to your questions. We do not sell uploaded document content to advertisers or other third parties. Avoid uploading documents containing sensitive personal, financial, or medical information you would not want processed by an external AI service.

4. Contact form

If you submit the contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent via EmailJS to our support inbox so we can respond to you. This information is used only to reply to your message.

5. Advertising and cookies

We display ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage your ad preferences at Google's Ads Settings page. We do not run push-notification ad networks, pop-under ads, or forced redirect ads on this site.

6. Analytics

We may use standard web analytics to understand overall traffic patterns, such as which pages are visited and approximate visitor location by country. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

7. Children's privacy

NKRHUB is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form on our homepage.
